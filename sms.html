Steady text messages

Steady by BrilliantWear uses text messages only for the purposes below. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to any message to stop, or HELP for help.

1. Sign-in codes

When you enter your mobile number in Steady and tap “Text me a code”, we send one message with a 6-digit code. Example:

Your Steady verification code is: 482913

2. Care-circle invites and alerts

If someone invites you to their Steady circle by your mobile number, we text you the invite link. If you add your mobile number in Steady and turn on “Text me urgent alerts”, we text you when someone in your circle may need you (for example, a possible fall they did not answer). Alerts never include health details. Examples:

Jeff invited you to their Steady circle: https://steady.brilliantwear.com/#/join?code=K7Q2M9XD — No password needed. Reply STOP to stop texts.

Steady: Jeff may need you. Open Steady: https://steady.brilliantwear.com/ — Reply STOP to stop texts.

Frequency varies with what happens in your circle; most people get a few messages a month or fewer.

3. News and offers (optional)

Only if you tick this separate box in Steady → Settings → Mobile number. It is never ticked for you and is not required to use Steady:

BrilliantWear: New Steady exercises are live — try them today in the app. Reply STOP to unsubscribe.

Stopping and help

  • Reply STOP to any message to stop texts from us. You can also turn texts off in Steady → Settings → Mobile number.
  • Reply HELP for help, or email support@brilliantwear.com.
  • Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Your privacy

We use your mobile number only for the messages above. We do not sell or share your mobile number or text-messaging consent with third parties or affiliates for their marketing purposes.

Steady shows everyday measurements and changes from each person's own usual. It does not diagnose, treat or predict falls. If someone may be hurt, call 911.
